# Official-authority coverage for company monitoring

Research checked **3 November 2026**. This is the source-selection standard for every registration country, plus jurisdictions where a watched company has material operations. A listed portal is a research candidate until its entity search, access conditions, permissible use and update frequency are tested. The current daily worker does **not** search these portals. See [registry.tsv](registry.tsv) for the existing inventory.

## Baseline authority types

| Priority | Authority or public record | Credit signals to capture | Scope and access check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Commercial / company registrar**, including state or provincial registrars | Legal name, registration number, status, incorporation, address, directors, capital, changes, dissolution, branches | Resolve the authority for the company's *actual place of registration*. Search by registration number first, then legal name and aliases. Check whether an extract or filing history requires payment. |
| 1 | **Tax and VAT authority** | Tax ID validity, active/inactive status, VAT registration, public tax debt or tax enforcement if disclosed | Public checks often validate identity only; confidential tax returns and balances are not presumed accessible. |
| 1 | **Official gazette / statutory notices** | Liquidation, capital changes, creditor notices, mergers, licence cancellations and legal notices | Include national and relevant state/provincial gazettes. Record the notice date and effective date separately. |
| 1 | **Commercial, civil and administrative courts** | Filed claims, injunctions, judgments, appeals, debt enforcement and regulatory challenges | Search dockets and published decisions separately, across relevant court levels and geographic jurisdictions. A filing is an allegation; a decision may be appealed. Subpoenas and sealed matters are often not public. |
| 1 | **Insolvency / restructuring register and insolvency court** | Bankruptcy petitions, administration, rehabilitation, moratoria, receivership, liquidation, creditor meetings | Some countries publish these in the company register or gazette instead of a separate register. For EU countries, the [European e-Justice insolvency search](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/registers-business-insolvency-land/bankruptcy-and-insolvency-registers_en) is a supplementary route; coverage varies. |
| 1 | **Securities regulator, stock exchange and official issuer disclosure system** | Audited accounts, interim results, material events, going-concern warnings, debt and covenant disclosures | Apply to listed issuers and regulated securities. Use the regulator/exchange source, not only the company's investor-relations page. |
| 1 | **Beneficial ownership / directors register**, where law permits access | Changes in control, owners, directors and disqualifications | Access and public fields vary widely. Preserve the source's definition of beneficial owner and effective date. |
| 2 | **Enforcement / bailiff / execution and secured-transactions registers** | Enforced judgments, asset seizures, pledges, liens and collateral releases | Usually separate from court decisions; some systems allow only parties or authenticated users. Include land and movable-asset registries when material. |
| 2 | **Sector licensing and supervisory authorities** | Licence grant, suspension, revocation, product recall, inspections, safety violations | Select by industry: central bank, insurance, telecom, energy, mining, agriculture, medicines, transport, etc. Also search the countries of material operations. |
| 2 | **General enforcement regulators** | Competition penalties, environmental orders, labour and safety findings, consumer and anti-corruption enforcement | Track whether a notice is an investigation, proposed penalty, final order or appeal. Include local regulators when they have jurisdiction. |
| 2 | **Public procurement and debarment authorities** | Contract awards/cancellations, non-performance, exclusion from tenders | Especially important when government revenue is material; include development-bank debarment lists where relevant. |
| 2 | **Customs, export-control and sanctions authorities** | Import/export restrictions, denied-party listings and licence limitations | Search local rules plus relevant cross-border regimes. [UN](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/un-sc-consolidated-list), [OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-list-service) and the [US Consolidated Screening List](https://www.trade.gov/consolidated-screening-list) provide machine-readable or downloadable screening data; a name hit requires identity review. |

For each source record: **country/region, authority, category, official URL, language(s), accepted identifiers, access mode, cost, update cadence, public-availability limits, last successful check and integration status**. The country source pack is incomplete if any priority-1 type has no identified source or a documented reason why it is not publicly available. A source listed in this folder is not automatically integrated into the daily worker.

## Daily search rule

At intake, confirm the legal entity using the company register and tax ID, then choose the competent courts, gazettes and sector regulators based on registered address and material operations. In each daily run, monitor sources that support lawful automated access; provide analyst links or manual check tasks for login-only and paid sources. Match registration/tax IDs before relying on a name match, retain original-language names and transliterations, and record source document, event date, publication date, case status and reviewer decision. Do not turn an inaccessible portal or an empty search result into a negative assurance.
