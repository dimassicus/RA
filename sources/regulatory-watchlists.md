# Regulator warnings and law-enforcement lists

Checked **3 November 2026**. These sources supplement the [official-authority checklist](official-authorities.md). Regulator warnings mainly concern financial-services activity; they are useful for a watched company, affiliate, owner or executive when the jurisdiction or activity is relevant. They are **not** a general solvency score. The RA worker now checks the FCA warning RSS for companies registered or present in the UK, matching the legal or short name in recent feed titles. A matching title is only a lead; the analyst must verify the legal entity and whether it is a clone. The worker does not yet query the full FCA historical list or the FSMA, FINMA and MAS lists. A public webpage is not automatically a free API or a licence to scrape.

**Priority official warning sources:** UK — [FCA Warning List](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms); Belgium — [FSMA warnings](https://www.fsma.be/en/warnings); Switzerland — [FINMA warning list](https://www.finma.ch/en/finma-public/warnungen/warnliste/); Singapore — [MAS Investor Alert List](https://www.mas.gov.sg/investor-alert-list). Use these when the watched company, affiliate or person has relevant operations or exposure in the jurisdiction.

| **FBI (United States)** | [FBI Wanted API](https://www.fbi.gov/wanted/api) returns structured public wanted-program records. | Public JSON API; candidate for owner/director screening, after terms and match rules are checked. A wanted listing is an allegation/active search, not a conviction. |
| **DEA (United States)** | [DEA fugitives list](https://www.dea.gov/fugitives/all), including drug and money-laundering allegations. | Public web; no official API verified. Low-frequency manual/reference candidate for relevant people. |
| **DIA** | If the US **Defense Intelligence Agency** is meant, its [mission is foreign military intelligence](https://www.dia.mil/about/faqs/), and no public wanted/warrant register was found. | **Do not add** as a wanted source without another expansion of “DIA” and a verified official endpoint. |

Implementation rule: search by legal name, known aliases, local script, website/domain and stable registration or person identifiers. Preserve the **issuing body, category, exact matched name, URL, publication date, current status and analyst decision**. Separate `warning`, `investigation`, `sanction`, `wanted`, `charged` and `convicted` states. Verify clones and namesakes before attaching any warning to a watched company or person; absence from a public list is not proof of authorisation or innocence.
