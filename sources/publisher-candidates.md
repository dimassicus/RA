## Implementation order

1. Keep the existing Google News discovery route for broad coverage, with publisher URLs retained for analyst review. It is not a substitute for a publisher licence or a guarantee of complete coverage.
2. Test the two El Comercio feed directories, Washington Post, LA Times, Independent and Japan Times for stable endpoints and appropriate business terms. Only activate direct ingestion when the terms permit the intended internal monitoring and report use.
3. Obtain commercial terms from Guardian, SCMP, Le Monde, Le Figaro, Al Bawaba, TASS and Wisers before connecting their restricted direct feeds or APIs. Compare their incremental coverage against the free discovery route before buying.
4. Resolve the exact identity of Latin Portfolio, Esmerk Latin America News, Latin America News and Moscow News before using those names as search filters.

No publisher-specific MCP server was verified. The concrete machine-access candidates found here are RSS, the Guardian and NYT APIs, or quoted commercial feeds. Website access alone is marked as a website lead, not a free API.
