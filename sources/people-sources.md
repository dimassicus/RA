## Practical procurement order

1. Integrate the **free official European Parliament and UK Parliament APIs** for relevant owners/managers and their roles, with person-identity review.
2. Pilot **one** commercial sanctions/PEP/negative-news product (WorldCompliance or Diligent/info4c) on a sample of the watchlist and key people. Ask specifically for API/batch rights, prices for daily re-screening, permitted corporate-credit use, retention and audit evidence.
3. If executive-movement coverage is still weak, compare [Nexis Data+ biographical data](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-in/products/nexis-data/data-sets/biographical-data) with a current structured executive product such as [The Official Board](https://www.theofficialboard.com/user/offre) (its public page advertises a free starter tier and US$99/three-month Premium, but API/redistribution rights need separate review). Do not infer rights from a reader subscription.

For RA, person records should store source ID, observed name and scripts, employer and role, dates, country, a stable official identifier when available, and **match confidence**. A biography match or warning should remain a lead until checked against company filings and the person's other identifiers.
