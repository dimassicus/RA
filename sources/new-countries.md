All are **research candidates**; the app currently uses GLEIF, BrasilAPI for CNPJ checks, and Google News RSS for preliminary intake. The sources below are not yet connected to daily monitoring. A public website does not imply a free API or permission to scrape, store or email full articles.

## Social and messaging leads

Use official company, regulator and publisher links to discover X/Twitter, Telegram and other accounts. Record the exact account URL, who links to it, last checked date, language, and whether it is official or an independent discussion channel. Do not infer account ownership from a similar handle. Social posts and chats are **unverified leads**: preserve the post URL and timestamp, then corroborate with filings, the company, or a reputable newsroom before reporting an event. [UzNet Press](https://uznet.press/en/) lists public Uzbekistan Telegram and RSS channels; it is an aggregator rather than an official register. [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/contact) publishes an editorial Telegram contact, which is not a news feed.

## Cost and integration priority

The linked government portals and most publisher websites are free to view, subject to site terms. No paid API price was confirmed for these six country sources, and no country-specific API is currently wired to the app. Start source-specific integration with issuer disclosures for listed companies, then local-language news feeds with suitable reuse terms, then national registry adapters. Keep paid extracts and publisher API licences as quoted/unknown until a price and use grant are verified.
