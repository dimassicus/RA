How to read these scores

Articles use integers. Weekly means retain two decimals; cell colour uses the mean rounded to the nearest integer, with halves away from zero. Every article has equal weight. The worst score and direction counts expose conflicting coverage. Repeated occurrences of the same exact URL count once; different articles about the same event still count separately.

Publication dates assign articles to weeks. Late discoveries and analyst corrections revise that week, while previous snapshots remain saved. The search count and source errors describe searches performed that week; historical articles can have scores without a search in their publication week.
