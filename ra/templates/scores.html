How to read these scores

Articles use integers. Weekly means retain two decimals; cell colour uses the mean rounded to the nearest integer, with halves away from zero. Every included event has equal weight within its publication week. The worst score and direction counts expose conflicting coverage. Repeated URLs, identical source documents and very similar attributed event sentences are grouped. Grouping can be corrected from an article’s review page.

Publication dates assign articles to weeks. Late discoveries and analyst corrections revise that week, while previous snapshots remain saved. The search count and source errors describe searches performed that week; historical articles can have scores without a search in their publication week.
