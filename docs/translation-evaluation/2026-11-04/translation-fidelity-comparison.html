The current app translates into English only. It completed 13 Russian → English cases; its other 13 cells are marked unsupported. NLLB and MADLAD completed all 26. Unsupported directions are not translation failures or zero scores.

Overall observations — open after reviewing the outputs

MADLAD was stronger than NLLB on several meaning-sensitive cases, but this set does not establish that it beats the current translator for Russian → English. The existing translator preserved the main negations, dates and amounts in the supported cases.

NLLB changed borrower to lender (N1_ru_en), confused a pledge/security interest with a deposit or guarantee (D1), omitted coupon (A1_en_ru), and produced an invalid currency name (A2_en_ru).

MADLAD still made material legal errors: pending became unsatisfied (L1_en_ru), and forgiveness of principal became repayment of principal (L2_en_ru). Literal HTML entities also appeared in several outputs.

All models preserved the main dates in the supplied examples. The date category still exposed legal terminology errors in NLLB. Correct digits alone do not prove the financial meaning survived.

These are provisional assistant observations on a small synthetic set, not a measured production error rate. Keep the running app unchanged; obtain human corrections, then test longer contexts and additional watchlist languages.

  • Candidate models: CPU, four threads, beam size four, source limit 480 tokens. Each input sentence fitted without truncation. Decoding-limit hits would be errors; none occurred.
  • Existing translator: the app’s current Argos pipeline and settings, including company-name protection. Its 1,000-character per-call cap was respected. Fallback downloads were disabled.
  • Every supported model received the identical source units. Matching corpus hashes and exact source coverage were checked. Complete input submission does not establish semantic completeness.
  • Russian and English cases were independently supplied source inputs, not round-trip translations. Neither model saw the other-language version or the checklist.
  • This tests short, deliberately selected sentences, not long documents or production sentence splitting. It does not establish coverage in Portuguese, Greek, Turkish or Vietnamese, or performance of downstream company attribution.
  • Models ran sequentially. Baseline settings differ, so runtime is not used to rank quality or throughput. No decoding parameters were tuned after seeing results.
  • The borrower and events are fictional. A translation judgment is not a credit score. Further analyst review and representative real-document tests are needed before changing the app.
